Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, including:

  • Username and email address
  • Password (stored securely using bcrypt hashing)
  • Financial transaction data you enter
  • Family member information you add

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process transactions and send related information
  • Send technical notices and support messages
  • Respond to your comments and questions

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt rounds of 10
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • We use HTTP security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options)
  • Two-factor authentication is available

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties except with your consent, to comply with the law, or to protect our rights.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, please contact us at our contact page.
